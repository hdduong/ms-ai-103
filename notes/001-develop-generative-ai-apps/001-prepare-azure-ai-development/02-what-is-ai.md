# 02 What is AI?

[What is AI?](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/prepare-azure-ai-development/2-what-is-ai)

- AI here means apps that look like they interpret input, reason, and generate a response. The models are machine learning models that store relationships found in large amounts of data.
- Pick the capability first. That decides which Azure service to provision.

| Capability | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Generative AI and agents | An LLM answers a natural language prompt, or an agent adds instructions plus tools so it can look up knowledge and carry out a task. |
| Natural language processing | Understand text: documents, email, social posts. LLMs cover many NLP tasks. Statistical NLP is still used for classification, sentiment, and summarization. |
| Computer speech | Speech to text, text to speech, live conversation. Handles noise, interruptions, languages, and accents. Also transcription and read-aloud. |
| Computer vision | Interpret images, video, and camera streams. Multimodal generative models can also create images and video. |
| Information extraction | Combine language, document understanding, vision, and speech to pull fields out of forms, receipts, images, and recordings. |
